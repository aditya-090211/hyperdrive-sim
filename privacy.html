Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026 · updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information Overdrive 009 ("Overdrive", "we") collects when you use this website, the HYPERDRIVE Simulator and Overdrive Scout (together, "the services"), how we use it and the choices you have. Overdrive 009 is a student robotics team competing in the National Robotics League (NRL). The services are fan-made and are not run by or affiliated with the NRL.

1. Information we collect

When you sign in. An Overdrive account is an email address and a password, shared by the simulator and Overdrive Scout. We store your email address, the name you give us and a username. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us. We send email only to confirm your address and to reset your password.

Scouting. When you use Overdrive Scout we store your team (number and name), the name you show to your teammates, your role on the team, the events your team creates, and the match and pit scouting reports you file. A report records what a robot did in a match (for example points scored, end-game result, ratings and notes) together with who filed it and when. We also record which version of the Terms and this policy you accepted, and when.

Simulator. If you are signed in, we store your settings and the results of full matches you finish. Online rooms share the name you type with the other people in that room; game traffic goes directly between browsers with a relay as fallback, and nothing from a room is stored.

On your device. Drafts, reports waiting to sync, pick lists, cached data and settings are kept in your browser's local storage so the services work offline. They stay on your device until you clear them, sign out or delete your account.

Technical data. Our hosting and database providers process IP addresses and basic browser information to deliver the services and keep them secure. We do not use advertising, analytics trackers or tracking cookies.

2. How we use information

  • To run the services: signing you in, saving and syncing reports, showing your team its data, and keeping the leaderboard.
  • To keep the services secure and to prevent abuse, such as forged scores or attempts to reach data you are not allowed to see.
  • To fix problems and improve the services.

Scouting and pit reports, events and team details submitted through Overdrive Scout are stored in one database operated by Overdrive 009. In addition to the team that submitted them, authorised members of Overdrive 009 can access this scouting data for every team that uses the service, and Overdrive 009 may use it for its own analysis, match strategy and alliance selection, and to improve the services. This is also set out in section 5 of the Terms of Use.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see your information

  • Your team. Everyone on your Overdrive Scout team can see the team's events, all of its reports, and the names and roles of its members, including who filed each report.
  • Overdrive 009, as described in section 2.
  • Everyone, only for the simulator leaderboard: your username, display name and best verified score.
  • Service providers who process data for us: Supabase (database and sign-in, hosted in Australia), Vercel (website hosting) and Google Fonts (typefaces, which your browser downloads from Google). They may only use it to provide their service.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account information until you delete your account. Scouting reports belong to the team that filed them: if you leave a team or delete your account, your reports stay with the team but are no longer linked to you. A team's events and reports are deleted when the team is deleted. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a short period after deletion.

5. Your choices

  • See and export your team's data with the CSV export on the Data tab.
  • Correct your name, your team's name or a report you filed at any time.
  • Leave a team from the Team tab.
  • Delete your account from the Team tab in Overdrive Scout or from your simulator profile. This removes your sign-in, profile, settings and match history straight away.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights, such as asking for a copy of your information or objecting to how it is used. Ask a member of Overdrive 009 at an NRL event, or through your team's mentor, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

6. Students and young people

Most NRL participants are students. If you are under the age at which you can agree to this policy yourself where you live, a parent, guardian or team mentor must agree for you. Please keep scouting notes about robots and play; do not include personal information about other people.

7. Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Access to every scouting table is enforced by the database itself, so one team cannot read another team's data, and all input is checked before it is stored. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes

We will update this policy when the services change. If a change is significant we will ask you to accept the updated policy before you continue scouting. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
