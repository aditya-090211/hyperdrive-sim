Our robot

Four wheels, one arm,a claw for COREs.

Drive
Four-motor skid-steer on 110 mm wheels
Frame
300 × 412 mm bumper frame, 21 mm off the floor
Arm
Servo parallelogram, 145.7 mm links, so the claw stays level
Claw
Two geared fingers that close around a CORE
Power
One 3S pack for all four motors
Control
HexaNRL firmware, 100 Hz loop

Traced from the model the simulator drives.

Every line here comes from our robot's CAD file, in millimetres. Move the arm: the parallelogram keeps the claw level from the floor to straight up.

The game

HYPERDRIVE,two and a half minutes.

Two squads, Red and Blue, two robots each. They play on a 12 by 12 foot field of 36 foam tiles. The last 30 seconds are LEVEL UP.

Match
2:30
LEVEL UP
30 s
Field
144 in
Robots
2 v 2

Rings on poles, a deck to balance.

Robots carry COREs, 95 mm rings, and thread them onto poles or drop them in a zone. Pick a row to find it on the field. Points are from the 2026 game manual.

What we made this season

Besides the robot, we wrote software for practice and scouting and opened it to every NRL team, and we started a health project for the Changemakers Award.

HYPERDRIVE simulator

The official field and our robot's CAD with real physics and live scoring by the 2026 manual. Play alone, with four drivers on one computer, or online.

Open the simulator

Overdrive Scout

Match and pit scouting on a phone, with rankings and pick lists. It keeps working when the venue Wi-Fi drops.

Start scouting

AI drivers

Rookie, Pro and Champion drive with the same controls and rules as a person, planning paths with A* across the field.

Robot code

Drop in a HexaNRL OpMode and it compiles in the browser and runs on a simulated control hub at 100 Hz.

Team tools

An Auto planner that writes the OpMode, a Driver trainer with timed drills, Alliance strategy odds from Scout data, and a Robot designer.

Our Changemakers Award project

Viora

A saliva test strip made from purple cabbage and filter paper, read by any phone camera. It tells you if you are dehydrated in thirty seconds, for less than a rupee.

Indicator
Anthocyanin from the cabbage changes colour with pH
Reading
Analysed on the phone. No photos are uploaded.
Evidence
Our first trial was small, one person, and the project site says so.
Read about Viora

Move or tap the ink. Blue means hydrated.
